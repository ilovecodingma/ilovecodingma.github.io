Korean Contests Open to Foreigners: How to Find and Enter

Published 2026-10-19

Korean public bodies, cities and companies run hundreds of contests (공모전) every month — photo, short video, essay, slogan, idea and design — many with cash prizes. A good number accept foreign residents. This page explains how to read the eligibility line and lists the open contests on this site that currently mention foreigners. The list below updates automatically every hour.

How to read the eligibility line (응모자격)

Korean wordingMeaningForeigners?
누구나 / 제한 없음Anyone / no restrictionUsually yes — confirm there is no nationality line elsewhere
내·외국인 누구나Koreans and foreignersYes
국적 무관Nationality does not matterYes
등록외국인 포함 / 국내 거주 외국인 포함Including registered foreigners / foreign residentsYes, if you have an alien registration card
대한민국 국민Korean nationalsNo, unless foreigners are added in brackets
○○시민 / 도민Residents of a city or provinceOften yes if your registered address is there

Step by step

  1. Find the 응모자격 line in the official notice and translate it word by word using the table above.
  2. Check the prize section (시상) for tax: prizes are paid after Korean withholding tax, and you will usually need your alien registration number.
  3. Check the submission format. Many contests accept entries in Korean only; video and photo contests often have no language requirement.
  4. Check the rights clause (저작권). Some organizers keep the rights to winning entries.
  5. If the wording is unclear, email the organizer before submitting. Keep the reply.

FAQ

Do I need to write in Korean?
It depends on the contest. Photo, video and design contests rarely require Korean text; essay and slogan contests usually do.
Can I enter as a team with Korean friends?
Usually yes, if every member meets the eligibility line. The team leader's details are often used for the prize payment.
Is an F-4 or D-2 visa a problem?
Contests rarely restrict by visa type, but receiving a prize is income; if you are unsure whether your visa allows it, ask the immigration contact center (1345).
