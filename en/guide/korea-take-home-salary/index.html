Korea Take-Home Salary 2026: Deductions, Rates and Examples

Published 2026-10-19

Your Korean contract states an annual salary (연봉, before deductions), but your bank account sees much less every month. This guide explains each deduction on a Korean payslip, shows 2026 rates with their official sources, and gives example take-home amounts so you can sanity-check an offer.

The six deductions on a payslip

Your employer uses the official simplified withholding table, so monthly tax can differ by a few thousand won; the difference is settled in 연말정산.

How to read your own payslip

  1. Find the gross (지급액 계) and the deductions total (공제액 계).
  2. Check pension: it should be 4.75% of your taxable monthly pay, but it stops rising once pay passes the KRW 6,590,000 base cap.
  3. Check health insurance at 3.595% and long-term care at about 13.1% of the health line.
  4. Non-taxable items (비과세), such as a meal allowance, are excluded from insurance and tax bases — ask HR which items are non-taxable.

Points that are different for foreign employees

  • National Pension: whether you must join, and whether you can get a lump-sum refund when you leave Korea, depends on your nationality and Korea's agreements with your country. Check with the National Pension Service before assuming either way.
  • Employment insurance: for many visa types it is optional rather than automatic — see our unemployment benefits guide.
  • Income tax: some foreign employees can choose a flat-rate option instead of progressive rates. Whether it is better depends on your salary and deductions; ask your employer's payroll team or the National Tax Service.

FAQ

Why is my first month's pay different?
Insurance can start mid-month and some employers deduct the first month's premiums later, so the first one or two payslips are often uneven.
Do I get money back in February?
Possibly. The year-end tax settlement (연말정산) compares tax withheld with tax owed after deductions such as card spending, rent and insurance. You get a refund or pay extra with your February or March salary.
Is the severance pay included in my annual salary?
Usually not. Severance (퇴직금) is paid separately after one year of work; some contracts include it in the annual figure, so check the wording.
