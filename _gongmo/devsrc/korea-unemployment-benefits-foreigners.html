Foreign workers in Korea can receive unemployment benefits (구직급여, often called 실업급여), but only if they were covered by employment insurance — and for most visa types that coverage is not automatic. This guide shows which visas are covered, how much the benefit is in 2026, and how to apply.

For E-9 and H-2, parts of employment insurance apply differently from other optional visas; confirm unemployment-benefit coverage for your exact case with the employment center (call 1350).

If your visa is in the optional group, check your enrollment now, while you still have a job. Time before the application does not count.

Step 2 — Do you meet the benefit conditions?

  • At least 180 days of insured work in the 18 months before leaving the job.
  • You left involuntarily (dismissal, contract end, business closure) or for a reason the law accepts, such as unpaid wages.
  • You are able and willing to work and actively looking for a job.
  • You apply within 12 months of the day after leaving; days left after that are lost.
  • You must still hold a valid stay status in Korea while receiving benefits.

Step 4 — How to apply

  1. Ask your former employer to submit the separation notice (이직확인서) to the employment center.
  2. Register as a job seeker on Work24 (work24.go.kr).
  3. Complete the online orientation for benefit applicants.
  4. Apply for benefit eligibility at the employment center that covers your registered address. Bring your alien registration card.
  5. Report your job-search activities on the assigned dates; benefits are paid after each report.

FAQ

I quit because the company didn't pay me. Can I still get benefits?
Unpaid wages are one of the accepted reasons for leaving. Keep the evidence (pay records, a labor-office complaint).
Will receiving benefits affect my visa?
Benefits do not extend your stay. Make sure your visa and job-seeking rules allow you to stay in Korea for the benefit period.
Can I work part-time while receiving benefits?
You must report any income. Unreported income is treated as fraudulent receipt.
