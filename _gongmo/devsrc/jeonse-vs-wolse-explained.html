Korean rentals use deposits that are far larger than in most countries, and the deposit is often the biggest sum of money a foreign resident has in Korea. This guide explains the three contract types, then the steps that legally protect your deposit — including the step that is different for registered foreigners.

Jeonse, wolse and ban-jeonse

TypeDeposit (보증금)Monthly rent (월세)Main risk
Jeonse 전세Very large, often a big share of the home's priceNoneLandlord cannot return the deposit at the end
Wolse 월세Small to moderateYesLower deposit risk, higher monthly cost
Ban-jeonse 반전세Large, but below jeonseYes, lower than wolseMix of both

In every type, the deposit is returned when you move out. The legal protection below matters more the bigger the deposit is.

Before signing

  1. Read the property register (등기부등본). Anyone can view it online at the Supreme Court Internet Registry Office (iros.go.kr). Check that the owner named there is the person signing, and look at the mortgage section (을구): existing loans are repaid before your deposit if the home is sold at auction.
  2. Meet the owner or check the power of attorney. If an agent or family member signs for the owner, ask for a power of attorney with the owner's certified seal certificate.
  3. Pay the deposit to the owner's account, not to a third party, and keep the transfer record.
  4. Use a licensed real-estate agent (공인중개사). Licensed agents carry professional liability insurance and must explain the property in a written statement.

Foreigner-specific checks

  • Report the new address on time. Registered foreigners must report a change of address within 15 days of moving (Ministry of Justice guidance). For deposit protection, file it on the day you move in.
  • Your visa end date vs. the lease end date. If your stay permit expires before the lease ends, ask for a clause that lets you end the lease early with notice, and plan how the deposit will reach you if you leave Korea.
  • Guarantee insurance eligibility. Conditions for foreign tenants differ by insurer and change over time. Ask HUG, HF or the insurer directly with your visa type before you sign, not after.
  • Overseas bank transfers. If the deposit comes from abroad, keep the inward remittance record; you may need it to send the money out again.

FAQ

Can I get the fixed date without the address report?
Yes, but priority in an auction needs both: the address report and the fixed date. Do both on move-in day.
The landlord asks me not to report my address. Should I agree?
No. Without the report you lose the right to stay against a new owner and your priority for the deposit.
Is jeonse cheaper than wolse?
It can be, because you pay no monthly rent, but your deposit carries risk and could earn interest elsewhere. Compare the total cost over the lease with your real numbers.
